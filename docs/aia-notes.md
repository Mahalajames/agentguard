# Canadian AIA Tool Notes

## Three Things to Borrow

1. **Simple questions:** Ask clear questions that are easy for nontechnical people to understand.
2. **Risk scoring:** Give each AI agent a risk level based on the user's answers.
3. **Clear results:** Explain the results so users understand what the risks mean.

## Three Things to Improve

1. **Focus on AI agents:** Ask questions about what an agent can do on its own and what tools it can access.
2. **Assess multiple agents:** Let organizations assess and compare several AI agents in one place.
3. **Give next steps:** Tell users what they should fix first and how they can make their AI agents safer.

## Summary

AgentGuard can use the Canadian AIA's simple questions and risk scoring while focusing more on AI agents and giving organizations clear next steps.
