About AgentGuard

Making responsible AI adoption easier to navigate.

AgentGuard is designed to help organizations understand the risks associated with AI agents and take practical steps toward stronger governance.

It brings together structured assessment questions, risk scoring, readiness indicators, and safeguards to support more informed decision-making.

A decision-support tool

AgentGuard is intended to support organizational review and planning. It does not guarantee that an AI system is safe, compliant, or appropriate for a particular use.

Assessment results are not legal advice, a certification, or a substitute for qualified human review.

Important: Assess AI systems, not individual people. Do not upload customer, student, patient, or other personal records.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is AgentGuard for?

AgentGuard is designed for organizations that use or are considering AI agents, including nonprofits, schools, clinics, and small businesses. It aims to make AI risk and governance concepts more approachable.

What if I do not know an answer?

The guided assessment includes an “I don't know” option where applicable. Unknown answers are kept distinct rather than automatically treated as zero risk.

Does a low risk score mean an AI agent is completely safe?

No. Scores are decision-support indicators based on the information provided. They do not guarantee safety or eliminate the need for ongoing monitoring and human judgment.

Does AgentGuard certify AI systems?

No. AgentGuard is not a certification service and does not establish legal or regulatory compliance.

What information should I avoid uploading?

Do not upload customer, student, patient, employee, or other personal records. Provide information about the AI system and its organizational controls instead.

How do I get started?

You can begin with the guided assessment or use the inventory upload workflow if you already have an AI inventory prepared.
